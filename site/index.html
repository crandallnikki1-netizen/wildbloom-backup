About Nikki

Created with purpose, intention, and a whole lot of heart.

House of the Wild Bloom was created by Nikki — a female veteran, maker, and founder who believes skincare should be both beautiful and purposeful. Every product is created with intention, using ingredients chosen for what they actually do, not just how they sound on a label.

What began as a passion for creating meaningful, small-batch products has grown into a ritual-focused collection designed to help you care for your skin, your body, and your space in a more thoughtful way. From facial ritual boxes to body care and candles, each item is made to feel special, useful, and rooted in purpose.

At House of the Wild Bloom, every ingredient has a purpose, every purpose creates a ritual, and every ritual is made to help you bloom.
